# Clearify Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-26

Clearify is a browser extension that analyzes news articles for bias and credibility. It is designed to collect as little as possible.

## What Clearify stores

Clearify stores only these, locally in your browser (via the browser's extension storage):

- Your AI provider API key (OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or NVIDIA)
- Your preferences (chosen model, settings)

This data never leaves your device except as described below. Clearify has no servers, no database, and no user accounts.

## What Clearify sends, and where

Only when you click Analyze on an article:

1. **Article text and headline** of the page you are on, sent to the AI provider you chose (OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or NVIDIA) to produce the analysis.
2. **Search queries about that story** to Google News RSS and, as a fallback, DuckDuckGo — used to find how other outlets cover the same story.

Nothing is sent automatically. Nothing is sent for pages you don't analyze.

## What we never do

- No ads, no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking pixels
- No profiles, no fingerprinting, no selling or sharing of data
- No browsing history: Clearify processes the current page only at your command

## Third parties

Data you send for an analysis is processed by your chosen AI provider under their privacy policy:

- OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
- Google (Gemini): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- NVIDIA: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/privacy-policy/

## Removal

Uninstalling the extension deletes the API key and all preferences from your browser immediately.
